# Daily Brief — Template

> The shape of a `/cos:brief`. Tune the sections to what actually helps you start the day.
> A good brief is short enough to read in under a minute and leads with something worth reading.

## Lead

> One thing worth your attention up top — a pattern, a heads-up, a win to carry in. Not a task. Not a calendar dump.

## Today

> What's actually on the calendar. Times, who, and — for anything that needs it — a one-line "here's what you'll want to have thought about first."

## Overdue / slipping

> Commitments and follow-ups that are aging. Each one verified as still open, not just pulled from a stale list.

## Prep

> Anything today that deserves five minutes of prep, with the prep already started (draft, notes, the relevant thread pulled up).

## One nudge

> A single suggestion, not a to-do list. The one thing that, if you did it today, you'd be glad you did.
